# Privacy Policy

**Bot:** Dungeons&Dummies
**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

This page explains what information the Dungeons&Dummies Discord bot uses and why.

## What the bot stores
To save your playlists and settings between sessions, the bot may store:
- **Server ID:** to know which playlists and settings belong to which server.
- **User ID:** to know who created or owns a playlist.
- **Playlist names and track lists** that you create.
- **Server settings** such as default volume.

## What the bot does NOT collect
- It does **not** read or store your messages. It only responds to slash commands.
- It does **not** record, store, or listen to anyone's voice. It only sends audio into voice channels.
- It does **not** collect emails, passwords, payment details, or any other personal information.
- It does **not** sell or share data with anyone.

## How the data is used
Stored data is used only to run the bot's features, like saving and loading your playlists.

## How long data is kept
- Data is kept while the bot is in your server.
- When the bot is removed from a server, that server's playlists and settings may be deleted.

## Deleting your data
To have your data deleted, open an issue at [github.com/gkuzmenko6/dungeons-and-dummies/issues](https://github.com/gkuzmenko6/dungeons-and-dummies/issues) with your server or user ID, or simply remove the bot from your server.

## Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
